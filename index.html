Mikhail Saleev

Hi, my name is Mikhail (or Misha)! I am currently a senior at the University of Notre Dame, majoring in Computer Science and Economics.

My research has primarily been focused on machine learning for remote sensing and chemistry. I build deep learning methods for underwater integrated sensing and communications, working under Prof. Toros Arikan. I also work on chemical reaction prediction with Prof. Xiangliang Zhang. More broadly, I am interested in AI for science, particularly in drug discovery and molecular biology. My methodological interests include generative modeling and reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on developing new learning and optimization algorithms.

AI for Chemistry

My work explores generative modeling for chemical reaction prediction through latent flow matching. I study continuous transformations of reactants in latent space over time. This includes predicting bond changes between reactants and products as well as examining how molecular structures evolve along learned flow trajectories.
